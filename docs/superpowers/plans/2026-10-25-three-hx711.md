# Three HX711 channels

**Goal:** One ESP running sketch_oct24a.ino produces three independent chart lines in Test & Recall, preserving existing controls and V3 single-channel support.

**Protocol:** USB 115200 baud. V5 PING signature is DEVICE,STRAIN_METER_3HX711_W5500,V5. DATA and READ have sample index, uint32 milliseconds, and three signed raw/delta pairs. STATUS,HX1=READY|BUSY,HX2=READY|BUSY,HX3=READY|BUSY describes conversion readiness, not connection loss. V3 signature and four-field DATA remain accepted.

**Approach:** Extract a transport-independent serial protocol decoder. Share one frame timestamp and maintain an EMA per channel. Extract a testable session model with per-channel baselines, calibration and stats; keep 30 seconds on the graph and all recorded rows. Render three colored filtered traces by default for V5 with optional raw overlays, retaining V3 raw/filtered rendering. Add a channel selector for the existing Live Data, strain and calibration controls. CSV/table add channel and firmware sample index so readings never become ambiguous.

**Constraints:** Preserve UI layout, controls, application/Particle DIC licensing and original firmware. USB scope; do not introduce a second UDP ingestion path. On disconnect stop recording; do not merge old and new device sessions. Missing or malformed frames must not update any channel. Firmware emits a complete frame only when all three sensors are ready.

**Review focus:** Channel/filter isolation; independent first-ten-sample baselines; calibration and inversion; malformed/partial frames; reset/reconnect/timestamp wrap; legacy protocol; three separate traces; CSV includes all channels and correct frame counts.
